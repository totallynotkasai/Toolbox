A look inside

Material You throughout, with real physics for the coin and dice.

Everything in the box

Tiles are grouped into categories, and anything you use often can be starred into Favourites. Search matches keywords too, so “decibel” finds the sound meter and “north” finds the compass.

casinoDecision Making

Shake your phone to decide: it works on all six.

CoinQuick flip, or Throw: a physics coin you swipe or flick. Keeps counts and streaks.
Diced4 to d20, up to six at once. Quick roll, or throw them across a walled table. Keeps your last 20 rolls.
Magic 8-BallAsk a question and shake for one of the 20 classic answers.
Random NumberAny whole number in a range, negatives included, with your last 20 kept.
SpinnerA wheel of up to 20 of your own options. A flick always turns it at least twice, so it can’t be aimed. Keeps your last 20 results.
Team SplitterSplit up to 60 names by team count or team size, then share the teams as text. Your last split is kept.

straightenMeasurements

Uses your phone’s sensors and microphone. Permissions are only asked for when you open a screen that needs them, and approximate location only if you turn on true north.

Bubble LevelTilt on both axes to 0.1°, which edge is high, and “Set as level” for a phone that doesn’t sit flat.
CalculatorBrackets, phone-style percentages, a live preview and exact decimals. Memory keys, your last 50 sums, and it turns with the phone.
CompassMagnetic or true north, with calibration and interference warnings and a marked bearing to follow.
Sound MeterApproximate dB with min, average, max and 30 s of history. Calibrate it to your phone’s mic.
Step CounterCounts from midnight even with the app closed. Goal ring, distance, calories and a 7-day chart.
Tally CounterTap to count anything, with undo after a reset.
TunerChromatic, guitar, bass, ukulele and violin, with a needle in cents.
Unit ConverterEleven categories, with UK and US pints, gallons, tons and mpg. Cooking turns cups into grams and gas marks into °C.

scheduleTime

Clocks and counters for the kitchen, the gym and the practice room.

Date CalculatorDays and working days between dates, adding and subtracting, and age.
Metronome30 to 250 BPM and tap tempo, 2/4 to 6/8 with accents, and 2, 3 or 4 clicks a beat. Remembers your settings.
StopwatchHundredths of a second and laps. Keeps running with the app closed, with Pause and Lap in the notifications.
TimerA real alarm: it rings on time with the phone asleep, at alarm volume, until you stop it. Countdown in the notifications.

Infinity CubeEight hinged cubes that fold open, roll over and close again, forever. It follows your finger and snaps shut with a click.
Purring CatStroke from head to tail to make it purr. Not the other way.
Rain StickTilt the phone to pour 200 grains past the pegs.
Rotary DialFine clicks, chunky detents, or a free spin.
Zip & RatchetZip it up, crank it down.

Small, private, made with care

Toolbox is built in Kotlin and Jetpack Compose, styled after Google’s own search tools.

palette

Material You

Picks up your wallpaper colours on Android 12 and later, with light and dark themes.

shield

Nothing leaves your phone

No internet permission, no analytics, no ads and no sign-in. Scores and settings stay on the phone, and go only into your own Android backup if you use one. Step history and the stopwatch are left out. True north keeps only the declination, never your location.

inventory_2

No third-party code

Pitch detection, audio synthesis, dice and coin physics and every game AI are written from scratch.

vibration

Shake to decide

Shake your phone to flip, roll, spin, ask, pick a number or reshuffle teams. Harder shakes throw higher.

accessibility_new

Works with TalkBack

Every screen reads out properly and fits at large text sizes, and the turn-based games can be played with TalkBack.

lock_open

Permissions on demand

Microphone and activity access are only asked for when you open a screen that needs them, approximate location only for true north, and notifications when you first start a timer or the stopwatch.

Install on your phone

  1. Download Toolbox.apk from the latest release on your Android phone.
  2. Open it. If asked, allow your browser or file manager to install unknown apps.
  3. Tap Install, then find Toolbox in your app drawer.

Needs Android 8.0 (Oreo) or later.

Upgrading from 1.0.0? It was signed with a different key, so uninstall it first. Its scores and favourites can’t be carried over. Later versions update in place.

Build it yourself

Open the project in Android Studio and press Run, or use Gradle with JDK 17 or newer:

git clone https://github.com/totallynotkasai/Toolbox.git
cd Toolbox
./gradlew installDebug
