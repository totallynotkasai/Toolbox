Small, private, made with care

Toolbox is built in Kotlin and Jetpack Compose, styled after Google’s own search tools.

palette

Material You

Picks up your wallpaper colours on Android 12 and later, with light and dark themes.

shield

Nothing leaves your phone

No internet permission, no analytics, no ads and no sign-in. Scores and settings stay on the device.

inventory_2

No third-party code

Pitch detection, audio synthesis, dice and coin physics and every game AI are written from scratch.

vibration

Shake to decide

Shake your phone to flip, roll, spin or pick. Harder shakes throw the physics coin higher.

star

Favourites and search

Star your go-to tools to pin them to the top, and filter by category with one tap.

lock_open

Permissions on demand

Microphone and activity access are only requested when you open the tuner, sound meter or step counter.

Install on your phone

  1. Download Toolbox.apk from the latest release on your Android phone.
  2. Open it. If asked, allow your browser or file manager to install unknown apps.
  3. Tap Install, then find Toolbox in your app drawer.

Needs Android 8.0 (Oreo) or later.

Build it yourself

Open the project in Android Studio and press Run, or use Gradle with JDK 17 or newer:

git clone https://github.com/totallynotkasai/Toolbox.git
cd Toolbox
./gradlew installDebug
